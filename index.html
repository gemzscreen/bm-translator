No readable text found

The scanner reads printed words. It can't read:

  • logos and brand designs (stylised letters)
  • very small, blurry, curved or shiny text
  • text taken from far away or at a steep angle

Move closer, hold the phone straight, use good light, and drag a box around the words.

Voice input needs internet. Offline, tap the microphone on your keyboard instead (Gboard / iPhone dictation), then the app translates the text offline.
